Good to know

Can I book a shoot that isn't listed?

Yes. The packages above are my most common sessions, but you can inquire about any type of shoot, including proposals, graduations, weddings, events, and nature or scenic sessions. Tell me what you have in mind in the inquiry form and I'll put together a custom quote.

How do I book?

Send an inquiry with your session type and ideal date. I'll reply to talk through the details, then confirm your date with a signed agreement and a retainer.

When will I get my photos?

Most galleries arrive within two weeks of your session. Larger or custom shoots can take a little longer, and I'll give you a date up front.

What if it rains?

We'll reschedule at no charge. I watch the forecast and will check in with you the day before.

Do you travel?

I'm based on the South Coast and happy to travel nearby. A travel fee may apply for locations farther out.
